Start a new garden?

Five new ducks hatch with new names and personalities. These five, and everything they have learned, their friendships and grudges, and whatever is lying in the garden, will be gone for good.

Micro Garden

Five small ducks, each running on its own live copy of a real fruit fly brain: 139,248 neurons and 2.7 million connections from the FlyWire connectome, simulated as spiking neurons right here in your browser.

They smell, see and hear. They get hungry, thirsty, hot and sleepy, make friends, hold grudges, and each has a personality. Tap a duck to watch its brain fire.

Starting

Sound on. The garden is saved in this browser, and the ducks are older by however long you were away.
